# Privacy Policy

**Bahá’í Prayer Book** (iOS)
Last updated: October 29, 2026

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

- **No accounts.** You never sign in or create an account.
- **No ads, analytics, or tracking.** The app contains no advertising, no analytics tools, and no third-party code that tracks you.
- **No data leaves your device.** Your bookmarks, bookmark lists, reading progress, recent prayers, recent searches, and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else.
- **No internet connection is needed.** All prayers are stored inside the app. The app only goes online when you tap a link (which opens in Safari) or choose to share a prayer yourself.

If you delete the app, everything it saved on your device is deleted with it.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
